Privacy Policy

Football Standings Predictor · Last updated 6 October 2026

Football Standings Predictor is made by Edd Davis, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: you don't need an account, the app has no ads, analytics or tracking, and the only thing you send us is any feedback you choose to write.

Information we don't collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account details. The app has no sign-in.
  • Your location, contacts, photos or files.
  • Advertising IDs or device identifiers. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Football data

To show league tables, fixtures and predictions, the app downloads football data from our server. These requests contain only what is needed to fetch the data, such as which league or team you're viewing. Nothing about you is sent with them. Our server gets the football data from a third-party sports data provider; your device never contacts that provider.

Feedback you send

If you use the feedback screen, we store:

  • Your message and the category you chose (idea, problem or other).
  • The app version and platform (for example Android), to help us fix problems.
  • A random ID created on your device the first time you send feedback. It isn't based on your device or anything about you. It lets the app show you your own messages and our replies, and it's lost if you uninstall the app.
  • When the message was sent, and any reply we write.

Please don't include personal details in feedback. We use feedback only to improve the app and reply to you. It is never sold or shared with anyone.

Server logs

Our server runs on Microsoft Azure. Like most online services, it keeps technical logs of requests (such as the time, the address requested and whether it worked), and the hosting platform processes your IP address in order to deliver the response and protect the service. We use these logs only to keep the app running and fix problems, and they are kept for a limited time.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a few settings on your phone, such as your preferences, the random feedback ID and when you last read our replies. This stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your information. Microsoft Azure hosts our server and database and processes data on our behalf.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Feedback is kept until you delete it or we no longer need it. You can delete any message you've sent from the feedback screen in the app, which removes it from our server straight away. If you've uninstalled the app and want feedback deleted, email us and describe the message, and we'll remove it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete information we hold about you, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
