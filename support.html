Football Standings Predictor

Football Standings Predictor shows whether a team is mathematically certain (or certain not) to win the league, go up, reach the play-offs, qualify for Europe, stay up or go down, with a plain-English explanation and example results.

Get in touch

The quickest way is from the app: open Preferences → Send feedback, choose Idea, Problem or Other, and write your message. You don't need an account, and replies appear in the same screen.

You can also email eddcjdavis@gmail.com. If you're reporting a problem, please say which device you're using and which team or league you were looking at.

Common questions

How up to date is the data? League tables and results are refreshed regularly from our football data provider. Preferences → About the data in the app shows the current schedule, and every screen shows when it was last updated. Pull down to refresh.

The app says the server may be waking up. After a quiet spell, the first request can take a little longer. Wait a moment or tap Try again.

Which leagues are covered? The Premier League, Championship, League One and League Two; the four Scottish leagues; and the Women's Super League and Women's Super League 2.
